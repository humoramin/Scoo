Scooter rental FAQ

What equipment is included with a scooter rental?

The included helmets and raincoats are shown on each scooter card. Contact us if you need help choosing helmet sizes or additional equipment.

How is the scooter rental price calculated?

The price depends on the scooter and rental period. Each card shows rates from one day through longer rental periods in CZK and EUR.

How can I request a scooter rental in Prague?

Select a scooter, click Rent Now and enter your contact details and rental dates. JustScoo will confirm availability using the contact details you provide.
